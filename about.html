About StudentStay

Finding student accommodation shouldn't be difficult. StudentStay brings accommodation options in Mankweng together in one place, making it easier for students to explore their options.

Our Purpose

StudentStay was created to make the process of finding student accommodation simpler and more accessible. We want students to spend less time searching and more time focusing on their studies.

How StudentStay Works

Students can browse available accommodation, compare monthly prices, explore facilities and contact landlords directly to make enquiries.

For Landlords

Landlords can submit their accommodation details for review. Once approved, their properties can appear on StudentStay for students to discover.
